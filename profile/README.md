# Simulation-Trained Vasomotor Policies for Drone Navigation

Welcome to the organization space for our final year research project on **simulation-trained vasomotor policies for drone navigation**.

## What this organization contains
- Core research code for training and evaluating vasomotor-inspired control policies
- Simulation environments and scenario configurations
- Experiment pipelines, logs, and ablation studies
- Benchmarking scripts and result analysis notebooks
- Documentation of methods, findings, and reproducibility notes

## Research focus
Our work explores how biologically inspired vasomotor-style policy mechanisms can improve:
- Adaptive flight control
- Navigation stability under uncertainty
- Generalization from simulation to real-world deployment

## Repository purpose
This organization is dedicated to **experimentation, iteration, and research collaboration** throughout the project lifecycle.
